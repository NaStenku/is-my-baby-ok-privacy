Privacy Policy

Baby Tracker

Tech for Human · Last updated: 2026-10-29

Who we are

Baby Tracker is provided by Tech for Human. For privacy questions or support, contact staiia.life.lab@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android closed-testing version with accounts, subscriptions and AI features disabled.

Records on your device

You may enter child profiles, including an optional name, date of birth, weeks at birth and country, and keep sleep, feeding, food, note and tooth records. These records and your app settings are stored locally. This release does not automatically upload your profiles or diary records to our servers or AI services. Older saved conversations may remain on the device after an update, but questions cannot be sent in this release.

Storage and security

Saved Android app records are encrypted, with the encryption key kept in secure device storage. Android cloud backup of app data is disabled. Encryption does not protect an unlocked device from someone who can use the app. We do not provide automatic cloud synchronisation in this release.

Backup and transfer

A backup is exported only when you choose Save backup. It includes all child profiles and records, including any older saved conversations, but excludes photos and sign-in credentials. Exported files are not encrypted. You choose where to save or share them, and the destination service may process the file under its own policy. Keep backups private. Importing adds missing records and preserves existing edits.

External websites and services

Source links open external websites. Those websites may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. The app does not attach your diary records to source links. Google Play may process installation, update and device information under Google's policies. This release has no advertising or developer analytics service, and does not activate account, payment or AI services.

Support messages

If you email staiia.life.lab@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and keep it only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending child health records or other sensitive information unless necessary.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app's stored data. You can delete an individual entry, delete a child profile, or use Delete all data in Settings. Uninstalling can remove local records; export a backup first if you want to keep them. Deleting records in the app does not delete exported files, copies you shared or support emails. We cannot recover local records or erase copies held by a service you selected. Contact us about information you have sent directly to us.

Parents and caregivers

The app is intended for adults who keep records about children in their care. It is not intended for children to use independently. Development and food information is general guidance. The app does not diagnose conditions or provide medical treatment.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before introducing features that change how information is processed, such as accounts, cloud services or AI questions. The date above identifies the current version.
